Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Novapex website and the information you give us through it. We collect little, we say exactly what it is for, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Who is responsible for your information

Novapex, based in Maseru, Lesotho. For anything about your information, email hello@novapex.agency.

What we collect

Requests and messages. When you book a free call or send the contact form, your answers are sent to us by email through Google, which hosts our email. If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to send.

Free guides. If you ask for a free guide, we keep your email address to send it to you, and to tell you about the Reply Kit.

WhatsApp tips. If you join, we keep your WhatsApp number to send you tips. Reply STOP at any time and we remove it.

Site visits. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people reach. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

Client work. If you become a client, the information you share during a Brand Launch, Page Management or Ad Campaigns engagement, including access to your pages and ad accounts, is covered by the confidentiality terms in your agreement with us, not by this page.

What we use it for

To reply to you, to send the result or material you asked for, to send WhatsApp tips if you joined, and to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. Nothing else.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not add you to any list you did not ask to join. We do not share your information with anyone outside Novapex except the service providers listed below, and only so they can do their job for us.

Where it is kept

Form submissions are stored in Google Workspace. Email is handled by our email provider. The site is hosted by Vercel. These providers may store information on servers outside Lesotho and South Africa, and each is bound by its own security and privacy obligations.

How long we keep it

Form submissions and email conversations are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you. Email hello@novapex.agency and we will act on it within 30 days.

How to delete your data

To have everything we hold about you deleted, including your WhatsApp messages with us, email hello@novapex.agency with the subject "Delete my data" and the number or email address you used. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Our WhatsApp assistant only keeps a short note of your chat (for up to 24 hours) so it does not answer twice; that note deletes itself.

If you are in South Africa, you also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator. Elsewhere, you can contact the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
