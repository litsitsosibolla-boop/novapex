The rule

We do not start here.

Every retainer begins with a Map, without exception. Selling you a monthly fee before anyone has established where your revenue is leaking is how agencies end up billing for activity, and it is the thing this firm exists to be the opposite of.

If you have a diagnosis from someone else that covers the same ground, bring it. We will work from it or tell you what it is missing.

Scope

What is in, stated plainly.

  • The fixes from your Map, built and installed in ranked order
  • The measurement that proves whether each one worked, before the next one starts
  • Content, where the Map showed content is what closes the gap, and never as the default
  • Direct access to the person doing the work, not an account manager
  • A quarterly review against the one number we agreed at the start

And what is not

  • Social media management as a standing service
  • Volume commitments. We do not promise a number of posts, because that is a measure of us being busy rather than you earning more
  • Paid media buying. We will tell you what to buy and hand it to someone who does it properly
  • Anything not traceable to a leak the Map found

Capacity

A small number at a time, and this is why.

The work is done by the person you meet. That caps how many engagements can run at once, and the cap is real rather than a device to make you hurry.

Novapex runs 3 engagements at a time. There is room for one engagement now. The next opening after that is November 2026.

The way in

The Map comes first. It always comes first.

Two weeks, a fixed fee, and a document you own. If you go ahead within 30 days, the fee comes off your first month, so the diagnosis costs nothing in the end.
