About Novapex

Marketing should not be a mystery
you pay for every month.

Novapex is a marketing team based in Maseru, working with businesses and leaders across Southern Africa. We plan it, run it and show you the results every month.

Why we exist

Too many businesses pay for marketing and never see what it did.

They post randomly. They get a report full of likes, or no report at all. When results are bad, the person they pay goes quiet.

We built Novapex to be the opposite: a plan before anything is posted, a plain report every month, and a team that replies and delivers on time.

What we believe

Three ideas behind everything we do.

01

A plan comes first.

02

Results belong in plain words.

Every client should understand their report without a dictionary.

03

Reliable is a marketing skill.

Replying fast and delivering on time builds trust, for us and for our clients.

Where we are going

The team Southern Africa trusts with its name.
