About

Attention is only worth
what happens next.

Novapex is a marketing systems firm. Most companies pay well to be noticed and lose the money after the inquiry arrives. Novapex exists to close that gap, with a method published in full and results measured in revenue.

What we believe

Three ideas the whole firm is built on.

Marketing should answer to finance. If it cannot be traced to revenue, it is a cost, not a system.

The leak is rarely the ads. It is the reply that came a day late, the lead nobody qualified, the quote that went cold.

Proof should come before payment. That is why the method is public and the first steps are free.

Where this is going

Owning the gap nobody is responsible for.

Companies across the region spend heavily on getting attention, and almost nobody is responsible for what happens after the call, the click or the message.

Novapex is building the firm that owns that gap, and the standard businesses use to judge whether their marketing is actually making money.

How we work

Small by design.

Every engagement runs the same four stages: Capture, Response, Qualification, Close.
