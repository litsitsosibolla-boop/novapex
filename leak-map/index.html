What you get

A document you own, whether or not you ever hire us again.

The Map is the product. It is not a sales deck with a proposal stapled to the back. It is written so that your own team, or any competent agency, could work from it without us.

  • A diagram of how an inquiry actually moves through your business, as it is rather than as the org chart says
  • Every point where inquiries currently die, named and located
  • A number against each leak: what it costs in lost close rate at your current volume
  • The fixes, ranked by payback rather than by effort
  • What to do in the first two weeks, written so someone can start on Monday
  • What we would not bother fixing, and why

What it examines

Four stages. The same four, every time.

Published in full, because a method you will not describe is a method you do not have. This is the whole structure.

STAGE 1

Capture

Does every inquiry get caught, and does anyone know how many arrived?

  • Every route an inquiry can take in, and the volume of each
  • Where each route lands, and who owns it by name
  • What happens to an inquiry outside working hours
  • Whether the monthly inquiry count can be produced on demand
STAGE 2

Response

How fast is the first reply, and does it move anything forward?

  • Time from inquiry to first human reply, measured not estimated
  • What the first reply actually contains and what it asks for
  • Whether follow-up runs by system or by memory
  • How many threads went quiet last quarter and were never reopened
STAGE 3

Qualification

Do you know who you are talking to before you spend an hour on them?

  • What is known about a buyer before the first real conversation
  • The written criteria for who gets time, if any exist
  • How often the answer is no, and who is allowed to say it
  • Whether loss reasons are recorded or only remembered
STAGE 4

Close

Does a good conversation reliably become a decision?

  • What happens in the hour after a good first conversation
  • Time from agreement in principle to a document in their hands
  • Whether close rate is known by source or only in aggregate
  • The gap between your best closer and your median one

How the two weeks run

Three hours of your time, spread across two weeks.

  • Week one, day one. A 60-minute session with whoever answers inquiries. Not the leadership team, the people doing it.
  • Week one. We follow real inquiries through your system, from arrival to outcome, using your own records.
  • Week two, day three. A 60-minute session to test what we found against what you expected. This is where most of the surprises land.
  • Week two, day ten. The Map is delivered in writing, followed by a 60-minute walkthrough.

The guarantee

It pays for itself or it does not get paid for.

If the Map does not find at least one quantified leak, the fee is refunded in full. If it does and you go ahead within 30 days, the fee comes off your first month.

Those two conditions cannot both fire. Either we found something worth acting on, in which case the fee comes off the work, or we did not, in which case you keep your money and we have told you something useful for free.
