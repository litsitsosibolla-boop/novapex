Published 27 October 20266 min read Novapex

The Reply Kit is a $197 set of copy-paste tools for owners who sell in WhatsApp and Instagram DMs. It covers the four places a chat goes quiet: the first reply, the price, the follow-up and the count. Five tools and one bonus, set up once, used on every inquiry after that.

Look back at last month's chats. Not the ones that booked. The other ones.

Someone asked if you had space on Saturday. Someone asked how much. Someone said "let me check with my husband and come back to you". You answered every one of them.

Then nothing. Nobody said no. They just stopped replying.

This post is about the Kit we built for exactly that. What it fixes, what is inside, what it costs and who should not buy it.

Why do inquiries go quiet when nobody said no?

Because going quiet is easier than saying no. Most people will not tell you your price was high, or that they found someone who answered faster. They just stop typing.

South Africans say this about themselves. The 2026 South African Customer Experience Report, based on 2,000 consumers, found that 55% would quietly move to a competitor after a poor experience, and 52% would stop supporting a brand without complaining publicly (as reported by Brand Spur on 1 September 2026).

So the silence is not a verdict on you. It is usually one of four small moments where the chat lost its next step:

  • The first reply was slow, or it answered the question and stopped there.
  • The price went out on its own, with nothing after it.
  • The follow-up never happened, because forty other chats came in.
  • Nobody counted, so the leak never showed up anywhere.
Nobody said no. The chat just lost its next step. Every quiet chat is a reply that ended without a question.

It fits if people message you before they pay. Salons, nails, lashes and barbers. Coaches, consultants, trainers and tutors. Clinics, therapists, wellness and fitness studios. Photographers, event planners, decor and catering.

It is not for you if:

  • You get no inquiries yet. The Kit fixes what happens after someone messages you. If nobody is messaging, you need traffic first.
