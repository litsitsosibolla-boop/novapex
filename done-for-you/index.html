What the Leak Map examines

Four stages. The same four, every time.

Published in full, because a method you will not describe is a method you do not have. This is the whole structure.

STAGE 1

Capture

Does every inquiry get caught, and does anyone know how many arrived?

  • Every route an inquiry can take in, and the volume of each
  • Where each route lands, and who owns it by name
  • What happens to an inquiry outside working hours
  • Whether the monthly inquiry count can be produced on demand
STAGE 2

Response

How fast is the first reply, and does it move anything forward?

  • Time from inquiry to first human reply, measured not estimated
  • What the first reply actually contains and what it asks for
  • Whether follow-up runs by system or by memory
  • How many threads went quiet last quarter and were never reopened
STAGE 3

Qualification

Do you know who you are talking to before you spend an hour on them?

  • What is known about a buyer before the first real conversation
  • The written criteria for who gets time, if any exist
  • How often the answer is no, and who is allowed to say it
  • Whether loss reasons are recorded or only remembered
STAGE 4

Close

Does a good conversation reliably become a decision?

  • What happens in the hour after a good first conversation
  • Time from agreement in principle to a document in their hands
  • Whether close rate is known by source or only in aggregate
  • The gap between your best closer and your median one
