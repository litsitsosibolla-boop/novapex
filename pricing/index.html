How we price

A clear quote, in your own currency.

Every business is different, so we quote after a free call, not before. The quote is in writing and in your currency: rands in South Africa, maloti in Lesotho, US dollars for international clients.

  • You see exactly what is included before you agree to anything.
  • Brand Launch is a once-off fee. Page Management and Ad Campaigns are monthly.
  • For ads, your own card pays Meta directly. Our fee and your ad money never mix.
  • You set a monthly ad spending limit. It cannot be overspent.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Do I need all three?

No. Some businesses only need Page Management. The free call tells you which step fits, and we will say so if you need less than you think.

Can I start with Page Management without a Brand Launch?

Yes, if your logo, colours and voice are already clear. If they are not, we will tell you, because a page built on a weak brand works twice as hard for half the result.

When do I see the plan?

Before anything is posted. You approve it first, then we do the rest.

How will I know it is working?

Your report arrives every month in plain language: what we did, what happened and what is next. No jargon, no guessing.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, straight to Meta from your own card. We work inside your ad account with full access and an agreed monthly limit, so your ad money stays yours.

Where are you based?

Maseru, Lesotho. We work with businesses and leaders across South Africa, Lesotho and the wider region, and online further afield.
