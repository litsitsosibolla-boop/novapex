The 20-Minute Leak Call

You describe how inquiries currently move through your business. We listen, and we name the single biggest leak we hear. You leave the call knowing the one thing to fix first.

No slides, no proposal, no follow-up sequence. If the answer is that you do not need us, that is the answer you get.

Why the form first

Twenty minutes is only useful if it starts at minute five.

The questions below take two minutes and remove the first ten from the call.
